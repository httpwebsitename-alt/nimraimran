Professional Biography

As a passionate Clinical Psychology professional with a Bachelor of Science (BS) in Clinical Psychology, my core objective is to create a safe, non-judgmental, and empathetic space for individuals navigating life's emotional and mental challenges.

My clinical background and academic continuous training empower me to understand complex psychological dynamics. Through specialized workshops and academic webinars—including trauma bond dynamics organized by the Youth Health Parliament Capital (YHPC) and academic stress management led by the Psyche Research and Wellbeing Society (PRWS)—I consistently refine my evidence-based approach to care.
